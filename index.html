How to use
Quick steps to get started

1. Patch your video — Open the Patcher tab, select your MP4, and hit Patch & Download. This rewrites the file's sample-table metadata so TikTok's pipeline treats it differently on the way in.

2. Upload — Once your patched file finishes downloading, the TikTok upload page opens automatically — just drop the downloaded file in there.

Pro tip: Need more screen space to work? Hit the Full Tab button on the Patcher screen to open the app in its own browser tab.

File size tip

Keep your source video under 150 MB for the cleanest results — TikTok compresses harder above that, even on a patched file.

Important

Videos uploaded to TikTok without patching will be automatically compressed by TikTok's system and cannot be displayed in HD quality.
